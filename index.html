Operations

Recruiting

  • Keep candidates moving so an open role does not stall.

SEO

Improve search visibility so the company is easier to find.

  • See which searches should reach the site, and which pages answer them.
  • Fix titles, page structure, and copy that misses those searches.
  • Make the site straightforward for people and for search engines.

AI integrations

Add AI where it improves efficiency, profit, or cost.

  • Find the repeated work a person still does by hand.
  • Connect a tool to the systems the company already uses.
  • Decide what stays with a person, and what the tool can take.
